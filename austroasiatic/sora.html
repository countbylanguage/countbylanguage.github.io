Sora

Austroasiatic > Munda > South Munda > Sora-Gorum > Sora

Sora is a Munda language with over 400,000 speakers, spoken by the Sora people in the Indian states of Odisha and Andhra Pradesh. It uses a base 12 or 20 number system. It has its own script, Sorang Sompeng, which was created in 1936 by Mangei Gomango.
